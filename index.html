01   /   The Story

Not built.
Grown.

Draft Synopsis

I came to heal, not to fight a kingdom. Then I found out the sickness was part of the plan. Now every answer I uncover pushes me toward a rebellion, a lie, or a war I never wanted.

Brevis once believed careful work and compassion could change a broken system from the inside. Then the city of Pyre taught him otherwise. The hospital he serves, the king he must answer to, and the rebellion gathering in the dark all want something from him - and none of them will spare the people he loves.

As Brevis rises through the palace’s medical ranks, he uncovers a hidden program designed to turn his own research into a weapon. The evidence points to his superiors, the order goes higher than he expected, and every step forward ties him more tightly to a choice he never wanted to make. Help the rebels, and he may lose the only access that could expose the truth. Stay loyal, and children keep dying.

Brevis wants a way to save lives without becoming the kind of man who kills for a cause. But in Machrinath, that may be a luxury he no longer has.

Lumi   /   Ferrofang Guardian Of The Caravan

A fierce kind of loyalty.

Taller than an adult, Lumi brings an apex predator's strength to the mountain road. Optical eyes, razor fangs, and piston-driven paws are part of her living anatomy, not equipment strapped onto an ordinary animal.

Steel-bright fur and small threads of foliage catch the daylight. Formidable as she is, this is a portrait of a guardian: alert, intelligent, and devoted to the people traveling beside her.

Liora   /   The Roots Of Leafshade

The courage to grow.

At Leafshade's adulthood ceremony, Liora touches a hand to her heart. Bright red flowers grow from her head, while silver facial structures and an articulated elbow belong to the same living body as her expressive eyes and gentle gesture.

Behind her, Brevis returns the gesture. Families gather among homes grown inside metallic tree trunks, while distant soldiers lend a quiet tension to the warmth of the ceremony.

Dahrek   /   The Shadow Of Nymphaea

A shadow over silver.

Above Leafshade, Dahrek sits astride a black nocturnis. Its leathery, batlike wings stretch around him, their metal ribs catching the light above the village's softly gleaming trunks.

The officer's blue uniform and reinforced silver plates stand apart from the living homes below. This is a glimpse of Nymphaea authority, set against a village whose doors and warm windows open directly into growing trees.

The Aquaflare   /   Wonder In The Shallows

Another life beneath the surface.

A long, narrow body undulates through the river, shifting from blue-green to purple as it turns. Almost-transparent fins stir the water, a luminous eye watches from a rounded muzzle, and a small trail of bubbles follows downstream.

Reflective scales meet flexible tissue and botanical channels. Here, even a quiet moment at the water's edge reveals the inseparable metal, plant, and flesh of Machrinath.

03   /   The Author

Ben Noble

Ben Noble is a computer engineer working in AI who has always had a heart for exploring the unknown. In his day job, he crafts creative solutions for previously unsolved problems; as a novelist, he applies that same curiosity to building worlds with entirely different rules and boundaries. Ben has spent years learning to weave compelling narratives to tell the stories that need to be told. THE REBEL OF MACHRINATH is the culmination of his desire to explore how life might be in a world of his own creation.

Off the page, Ben lives with his wife, Amy, whom he married in 2013, and their three children: Ivy (born 2018), Felix (born 2019), and Gene (born 2023).
