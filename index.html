01   /   The Story

Not built.
Grown.

Draft Synopsis
Based on the premise. Final story details to come.

On Machrinath, there is no clean line between the living and the made.

Metal grows in the wild. Copper runs through living forms, and the machinery of the world belongs to nature itself. What looks like a forest may also be a mechanism; what looks like a machine may be very much alive.

Here, an ancient-looking world holds an extraordinary kind of technology. Its wonders are not separate from its wilderness. They are rooted in it, shaped by the same forces that sustain the people who call Machrinath home.

The Rebel of Machrinath brings speculative science fiction and young adult fantasy into a world of organic technology, strange beauty, and the possibility of change. At its heart is a simple question: when everything around you is connected, what might a single spark set in motion?

03   /   The Author

BN.

Ben Noble

Ben Noble is a computer engineer working in AI who has always had a heart for exploring the unknown. In his day job, he crafts creative solutions for previously unsolved problems; as a novelist, he applies that same curiosity to building worlds with entirely different rules and boundaries. Ben has spent years learning to weave compelling narratives to tell the stories that need to be told. THE REBEL OF MACHRINATH is the culmination of his desire to explore how life might be in a world of his own creation.

Off the page, Ben lives with his wife, Amy, whom he married in 2013, and their three children: Ivy (born 2018), Felix (born 2019), and Gene (born 2023).
